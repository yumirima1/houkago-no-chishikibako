# Observer Room assets

Retrieved from Kenney's official distribution on 2026-10-26. Both original
license files are retained alongside the GLBs. Total models, texture and original
licenses: 610,976 bytes before this attribution file. No third-party CDN is
contacted at runtime.

## Furniture Kit — Kenney — CC0

- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit
- Download: https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/furniture-kit/440e0608a4-1677580847/kenney_furniture-kit.zip
- License: furniture/LICENSE.txt; https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Models: televisionVintage, desk, computerKeyboard, computerMouse,
  lampRoundTable, books, plantSmall1, bookcaseOpen.
- Adaptation: televisionVintage is used as the deep CRT housing. Material colors
  and model proportions are changed in the scene, with additional curved glass,
  stand, vents, keys and power indicator. Desk wood uses the existing project's
  procedural texture. Decorative notebook, telephone and wall papers are local
  geometry. Display artwork is generated locally and is decorative, not live data.

## City Kit Commercial 2.1 — Kenney — CC0

- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial
- Download: https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/city-kit-commercial/a742d900eb-1753115042/kenney_city-kit-commercial_2.1.zip
- License: city/LICENSE.txt; https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Models: building-c, building-f, building-skyscraper-b, building-skyscraper-e,
  low-detail-building-a, low-detail-building-d.
- Adaptation: darkened materials, adjusted proportions, shared instanced window
  lights. Train and elevated track are lightweight local geometry.

Furniture GLBs: 3,476–23,132 bytes each. City GLBs: 8,716–148,952 bytes each.
Furniture GLBs include materials. City GLBs share the locally bundled
city/Textures/colormap.png (11,002 bytes). No remote asset URI is needed.
There is one 1024×1024 shadow map, a capped device-pixel ratio of 1.5, and no
post-processing pipeline. Actual GPU performance still needs browser measurement.

The pre-existing ../ATTRIBUTION.md describes the earlier forest room. It is
retained as historical documentation; it does not describe this new scene.
